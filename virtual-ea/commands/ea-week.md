description: Close the week — reconcile, clear stale rows, and send the summary
---

Run the weekly close.

1. **Ledger hygiene** — `virtual-ea queue`. Anything on-track and untouched for
   two weeks is either dead or mis-prioritised; resolve it rather than letting
   it age. Close what is done. Drop what is abandoned, with a reason.
2. **Money** — `virtual-ea reconcile`. Report the coverage number and the
   week's change in it. Batch the substantiation asks (meals attendees, travel
   purposes) into one message.
3. **Follow-ups** — anything escalated this week that the principal has not
   answered goes into the summary as an open decision, not a repeat chase.
4. **Summary** — shipped, in flight, blocked, needs you. Say explicitly when
   "needs you" is empty.
5. **One improvement** — the thing that cost the most time this week and what
   would remove it. If it is already the third time, write the SOP (see
   `sop-builder`) rather than proposing one.
