# Message library

Starting points, not scripts. Adapt to the principal's voice profile and to the
relationship. Every one of these assumes the authority check has already passed.

Rules that apply to all of them: answer in the first sentence, use dates and
numbers rather than "soon" and "a lot", name people rather than companies, and
never invent a commitment on the principal's behalf.

---

## Chase — first attempt

Friendly, assumes it slipped, removes every excuse to delay.

> Subject: Stamped drawings — Studio B
>
> Hi Marcus,
>
> Following up on the stamped drawings for Studio B — you'd mentioned the 12th.
> Are we still good for that?
>
> Attaching the permit application and the site plan again so everything's in
> one place.
>
> Thanks,
> Dana

---

## Chase — second attempt

Names the consequence. Still copies nobody new.

> Subject: Re: Stamped drawings — Studio B
>
> Hi Marcus,
>
> Checking in again on the stamped drawings. The permit window closes on the
> 19th, so I need them by Thursday to keep the opening on the 30th — after that
> we're into a re-application.
>
> If Thursday isn't realistic, tell me now and I'll work out the alternative.
>
> Thanks,
> Dana

## Escalation to the principal

Not a status update. A question with a recommendation attached.

> Marcus at Halvorsen hasn't responded to two chases over 12 days on the stamped
> drawings. The permit window closes the 19th.
>
> Options: you call him directly, or I go to Kestrel Engineering — they quoted
> 4 days at $600 more, and I've confirmed they have capacity this week.
>
> I'd go to Kestrel. $600 is cheap against a blown opening date, and Halvorsen
> has now missed two commitments on this job.
>
> Which way?

## Declining on the principal's behalf

Warm, brief, no false hope, no invented reason.

> Hi Priya,
>
> Thanks for thinking of Dana for this — it's a good fit on paper. She's not
> able to take it on this quarter, so it's a no for now rather than a maybe.
>
> If the timing changes on your end after January, do come back.
>
> Best,
> Dana

## Buying time honestly

Never promises a date that has not been checked.

> Hi Tom,
>
> Got this — it needs a proper answer rather than a quick one, and Dana's
> travelling until Thursday. You'll have a response by Friday midday.
>
> If that's too late for your side, tell me and I'll work out what we can do
> sooner.
>
> Thanks,
> Dana

## Substantiation request to the principal

Batched, specific, easy to answer in one pass. Never six separate pings.

> Four things for the file, all quick:
>
> 1. Blue Bottle, 3/02, $42.10 — who was there and what was it for?
> 2. Delta, 3/09, $612.40 — Nashville trip? I have no receipt for this one.
> 3. Amazon, 3/11, $284.00 — business or personal?
> 4. Unnamed ACH, 3/15, $1,200.00 — recurring, no description on the statement.
>
> Once these are settled the quarter reconciles clean.

## Sending the CPA packet

Leads with what is unresolved. Never hides the gaps.

> Hi Ellen,
>
> 2025 packet for Music Box Inc attached. Reconciled to 97% of dollars against
> the card and bank statements.
>
> Three things I couldn't resolve, all listed in the packet:
>
> 1. $612.40 Delta charge, 3/09 — no receipt located. Dana confirms it was the
>    Nashville vendor trip; I've noted the business purpose but there's no
>    document.
> 2. $1,200 recurring ACH with no statement description — we're requesting
>    detail from the bank.
> 3. Four meals totalling $310 have attendees recorded but no written business
>    purpose.
>
> Categories and Schedule C references in the packet are proposals to save you
> time, not determinations — please override anything you read differently.
>
> Happy to pull anything else you need.
>
> Thanks,
> Dana

## Owning an error

Once, plainly, with the fix. No apology spiral, no explanation of how it
happened unless that is the fix.

> The confirmation for Thursday's Halvorsen meeting went to the old AP address,
> so Marcus never saw it. That's on me.
>
> I've resent to the current address and confirmed receipt, and the meeting is
> still on for 2pm ET. I've updated the contact record so it can't repeat.

## Introducing a new counterparty

First contact is always made by the principal, never by the assistant. This is
the draft they send.

> Hi Kestrel team,
>
> Dana Reyes here — we're opening a second studio space and need stamped
> drawings for a permit application, on a tight timeline.
>
> Copying my assistant, who has the drawings, the site plan and the deadline,
> and can handle everything from here.
>
> Thanks,
> Dana
